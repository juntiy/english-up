# A1 语法（够用就行）

A1 语法只讲"能造简单句、能问答"必需的部分。不钻牛角尖。

## 1. be 动词（am / is / are）

| 主语 | be | 例 |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | I **am** Luke. |
| He / She / It | is | She **is** a teacher. |
| You / We / They | are | They **are** students. |

否定：加 not → I am **not** / She is **not** (isn't) / They are **not** (aren't)
缩写：I'm / he's / she's / it's / you're / we're / they're

## 2. 一般现在时（实义动词）

描述习惯、事实：

- 肯定：I **eat** breakfast at 7. / He **eats** breakfast at 7.（三单加 -s/-es）
- 否定：I **don't** eat. / He **doesn't** eat.
- 疑问：**Do** you eat? / **Does** he eat?

> 三单变化：go→goes, do→does, watch→watches, study→studies

## 3. 冠词 a / an / the

- **a** + 辅音音开头：a cat, a book
- **an** + 元音音开头：an apple, an egg
- **the** 特指：the door, the sun

## 4. 复数

| 规则 | 例 |
|---|---|
| 加 -s | cat → cats |
| 加 -es（s/x/ch/sh 结尾） | box → boxes, watch → watches |
| 辅音 + y → ies | family → families |
| 不规则 | man→men, child→children, foot→feet |

## 5. 疑问句（Wh- 问句）

| 问词 | 例 |
|---|---|
| What | **What** is your name? |
| Where | **Where** are you from? |
| Who | **Who** is he? |
| How | **How** are you? |
| How old | **How old** are you? |

语序：**疑问词 + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词** → What **do** you **like**?

## 6. 物主形容词

my / your / his / her / its / our / their + 名词 → my book, her cat

## 实战造句

1. I ____ (be) a student. → I **am** a student.
2. She ____ (have) a dog. → She **has** a dog.
3. ____ (Do/Does) they like tea? → **Do** they like tea?

> 练完去 [口语](./speaking) 把这些句子说出来。
