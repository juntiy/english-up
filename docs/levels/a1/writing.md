# A1 写作（从填空到小句子）

A1 写作不强求长文，先把"正确的简单句"写稳。

## 1. 句型填空（先模仿）

用 am / is / are 填空：

1. I ___ a student.
2. She ___ my sister.
3. They ___ happy.
4. The cat ___ on the table.

<details><summary>答案</summary>1. am　2. is　3. are　4. is</details>

## 2. 改写句子（肯定 → 否定）

例：I am a teacher. → I **am not** a teacher.
1. He is tall. → He ___ tall.
2. They are friends. → They ___ friends.

<details><summary>答案</summary>1. is not (isn't)　2. are not (aren't)</details>

## 3. 写 3 句关于自己

用模板写（参考 [口语](./speaking)）：

> My name is ______.
> I am from ______.
> I like ______.

## 4. 写一封极简邮件/留言

> Hi Anna,
> I am Tom. I am from China. I like music.
> See you!

## 写作检查清单

- [ ] 主语和 be 动词匹配（I am / he is）
- [ ] 三单动词加了 -s（He likes ...）
- [ ] 句首大写、句尾有标点
- [ ] 用对 a / an（a dog, an egg）

> 写完后去 [自测](./checkpoint) 收尾。
