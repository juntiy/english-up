# B1 口语：表达观点与经历

B1 口语从「描述」升级到「表达」——你能说出**自己的想法、理由、经历和假设**。

## 模板 1：表达观点 + 理由

> "I think ___ because ___. In my opinion, ___. For example, ___."

**练习**：I **think** we **should** take the train **because** it's cheaper. **For example**, a ticket is only 20 yuan.

## 模板 2：描述经历（完成时）

> "I have (never) ___ . The best experience was when I ___."

**练习**：I **have traveled** to three countries. The best experience **was** when I **ate** street food in Bangkok.

## 模板 3：条件句谈假设

> "If I had more time, I would ___."

**练习**：If I **had** more time, I **would learn** to play guitar.

## 高频功能句（直接套）

- **赞同**：I totally agree. / That makes sense.
- **反对**：I see your point, but... / I'm not sure about that.
- **犹豫**：Well, it depends. / Let me think.
- **建议**：Why don't we...? / How about...?
- **澄清**：Sorry, what do you mean by...?

## 辩论小练习（自言自语 1 分钟）

话题：*Should students use phones in class?*
- 立场句：I think students **shouldn't** use phones in class **because** they **are distracted**.
- 让步句：However, phones **can be** useful for research.
- 结论句：So they **should be** allowed only for learning.

## 自练方法

1. 每天挑 1 个话题，用「观点 + 理由 + 例子」说 1 分钟。
2. 录音回听，标记「该用完成时却用了过去时」的地方。
3. [学习中心](/) 闯关强制开口跟读，巩固语感。

::: tip 流利度优先
B1 不要求零错误，要求「能连贯表达复杂一点的事」。先敢说，再纠细节。
:::
