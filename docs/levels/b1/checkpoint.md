# B1 自测过关

答对 **80% 以上** 即确认掌握 B1，进入 B2。答案在底部。

## 一、现在完成时

1. I ___ (never / be) to New York.
2. She ___ (just / finish) her homework.
3. We ___ (live) here for 10 years.

## 二、被动语态（改写）

4. Someone stole my bike. → My bike ___ ___ (steal).
5. They built this bridge in 1990. → This bridge ___ ___ in 1990.
6. People speak English worldwide. → English ___ ___ worldwide.

## 三、条件句

7. If it ___ (rain), we will stay home. （真实）
8. If I ___ (be) you, I would apologize. （假设）
9. If he ___ (study) harder, he would pass. （假设）

## 四、定语从句

10. The woman ___ (who/which) called you is my boss.
11. The book ___ (who/which) I read was excellent.

## 五、写作（写一段 4–5 句）

12. 用 B1 段落结构写：*Why is exercise important?*（必须含 1 个完成时 + 1 个连接词）

## 六、口语自测（说 1 分钟）

13. 用完成时讲一段你的经历（*I have... The best was when...*）。
14. 用条件句谈一个假设（*If I had..., I would...*）。

---

## 参考答案

**一**
1. have never been　2. has just finished　3. have lived

**二**
4. was stolen　5. was built　6. is spoken

**三**
7. rains（条件句 if 后不用 will）　8. were（Type 2 用 were）　9. studied（Type 2 过去时）

**四**
10. who（人）　11. which（物）

**五** 示例：
> Exercise **has become** important to me. **First**, it keeps me healthy; **for example**, I rarely get sick now. **However**, it takes time. **In conclusion**, the benefits are worth it.

**六** 自由表达，能流利使用目标结构即可。
