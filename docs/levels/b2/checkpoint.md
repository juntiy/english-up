# B2 自测过关

答对 **80% 以上** 即确认掌握 B2，进入 C1+。答案在底部。

## 一、虚拟语气

1. If I ___ (know) the answer, I would tell you. （与现在相反）
2. If she ___ (study) harder, she would have passed. （与过去相反）
3. I suggest that he ___ (leave) early. （建议后）

## 二、倒装

4. Never ___ I (see) such a beautiful sunset.
5. Only after the meeting ___ we (realize) the mistake.

## 三、非谓语

6. ___ (Live) in Beijing, I see crowds every day.
7. The book ___ (write) in 1990 is still popular.

## 四、长句拆解

8. 划出主干：*The study, which took five years, shows that sleep, despite busy lives, matters.*

## 五、写作（写一段议论文，5–6 句）

9. 题目：*Should cities ban cars in the center?* 必须含：thesis + 1 让步段 + 1 倒装/强调句。

## 六、口语自测（说 2 分钟）

10. 用 PREP 法就「远程办公的利弊」做即兴演讲，含 1 个委婉反驳 + 1 个习语。

---

## 参考答案

**一**
1. knew　2. had studied　3. (should) leave

**二**
4. have I seen（否定词开头倒装）　5. did we realize（Only 开头倒装）

**三**
6. Living（主动进行）　7. written（被动完成）

**四**
主干：The study shows that sleep matters.（which took five years 定语；despite busy lives 状语）

**五** 示例：
> *I argue that city centers should ban cars.* **Firstly**, it cuts pollution; *for instance*, Madrid saw cleaner air. **Admittedly**, shops fear lost customers; *however*, pedestrians boost local business. **It is public health that matters most.** Therefore the ban is justified.

**六** 自由表达，能流利用目标结构即可。
