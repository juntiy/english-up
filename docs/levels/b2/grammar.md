# B2 语法：长难句、虚拟、倒装

B2 语法为**读写硬门槛**服务：你能拆解别人写的复杂句，也能写出自己的复杂句。

## 1. 长难句拆解（核心技能）

遇到长句，三步拆：
1. **找主干**：主语 + 谓语 + 宾语（忽略修饰）。
2. **拆修饰**：which/that/who 引导的从句、介词短语、分词。
3. **连逻辑**：因果、转折、递进。

**示例**：
> *The report, **which was published yesterday**, **reveals** that emissions, **despite government efforts**, **have risen**.*

- 主干：The report reveals that emissions have risen.
- 修饰：which was published yesterday（定语）; despite government efforts（状语）.

## 2. 非谓语动词（-ing / -ed / to do）

- **Doing** ..., he ... （主动进行）：*Living in the city, I see crowds daily.*
- **Done**, ... （被动完成）：*Written in 1990, the book is classic.*
- ... **to do** （目的）：*He came to borrow my book.*

## 3. 虚拟语气（Subjunctive）

**与现在/事实相反**：if + did, would do
- *If I **were** you, I **would accept**.*（不用 was，正式用 were）

**与过去相反**：if + had done, would have done
- *If I **had known**, I **would have helped**.*（后悔类）

**建议/要求后**：(suggest/demand/insist) that + (should) do
- *I suggest that he **(should) leave**.*

## 4. 倒装（Inversion，强调/书面）

- 否定词开头：*Never **have I seen** such a thing.*
- Only 开头：*Only then **did I realize**.*
- 地点开头：*On the hill **stands** a tower.*

## 5. 强调句（Cleft）

- 普通：*I met him yesterday.*
- 强调：*It **was** yesterday **that** I met him.*
- 强调人：*It **was** him **who** I met.*

::: warning 易错
虚拟语气 if 后用 were 不用 was（正式写作）；与过去相反用 had done，不是 did。这是 B2 写作最容易扣分的点。
:::

::: tip 练习法
每天找 1 句新闻长句，用「找主干→拆修饰」法拆解；再自己仿写 1 句含非谓语/虚拟的句子。30 天形成肌肉记忆。
:::
