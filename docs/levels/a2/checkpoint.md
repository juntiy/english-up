# A2 自测过关

完成下面的自测，答对 **80% 以上** 即可确认掌握 A2，进入 B1。答案在页面底部，先独立完成再看。

## 一、时态填空（过去 / 将来）

1. Yesterday I ___ (go) to the supermarket.
2. She ___ (eat) an apple this morning.
3. Tomorrow I ___ (visit) my grandparents.
4. Look at the clouds! It ___ (rain).
5. We ___ (watch) a movie last night.

## 二、比较级

6. This book is ___ (cheap) than that one.
7. My phone is ___ (expensive) than yours.
8. He is the ___ (tall) boy in the class.

## 三、情态动词

9. You ___ (should / will) see a doctor if you feel sick.
10. I ___ (can / must) swim very well.

## 四、连词成句（用过去时讲一件小事）

11. 把这三个词连成一句：*yesterday / I / buy / a cake*
12. 把这三个词连成一句：*we / go / beach / happy*

## 五、口语自测（对着镜子说 30 秒）

13. 用 3 句话讲你上周末做了什么（必须含过去式）。
14. 用 2 句话讲你明天打算做什么（必须含 going to / will）。

---

## 参考答案

**一**
1. went（不规则）　2. ate（不规则）　3. will visit / am going to visit　4. is going to rain（有迹象）　5. watched（规则 -ed）

**二**
6. cheaper（短形容词 + -er）　7. more expensive（长词用 more ... than）　8. tallest（最高级 the + -est）

**三**
9. should（建议）　10. can（能力）

**四**
11. Yesterday I **bought** a cake.
12. We **went** to the beach and were happy. （或 We went to the beach; we were happy.）

**五**
示例：*Last weekend I **cleaned** my room and **cooked** dinner. It **was** tiring but fun.*
示例：*Tomorrow I **am going to** read a book. After that I **will** sleep early.*
