# A2 口语：讲清一件小事

A2 口语的标志是**能连贯地说出过去和未来的事**。下面给三套可直接套用的模板。

## 模板 1：描述过去经历（Past）

> "Last weekend, I **went** to ___ with ___. We **___ed** and **___ed**. It **was** ___."

**填空练习**：
- Last weekend, I **went** to the **beach** with my **family**.
- We **swam** and **ate** seafood.
- It **was** very **relaxing**.

## 模板 2：计划未来（Future）

> "Tomorrow / Next ___, I **am going to** ___. After that, I **will** ___."

**填空练习**：
- Tomorrow I **am going to** **clean** my room.
- After that, I **will** **watch** a movie.

## 模板 3：比较与建议

> "A is **better / cheaper / faster** than B. So I think you **should** ___."

**填空练习**：
- The bus is **cheaper** than the taxi.
- So I think you **should** take the bus.

## 高频口语短语（背下来直接说）

- How was your weekend? — It was great / not bad.
- What did you do? — I went to...
- What are you going to do? — I'm going to...
- Sounds good! / That's a good idea.
- I'd like to... / I prefer...

## 自练方法

1. 每天选 1 个模板，用自己真实的事填空，说 30 秒。
2. 用手机录音，回听找「过去式有没有漏」。
3. 进 [学习中心](/) 点「开始今日学习」，闯关里有跟读环节强制开口。

::: tip 别怕错
A2 阶段「能说出来、时态大致对」远比「完美语法」重要。先流利，再准确。
:::
